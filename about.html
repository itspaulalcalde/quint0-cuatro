INTRODUCTION / 001

Hi, I'm Paula.

A graphic and multimedia designer interested in creating thoughtful visual experiences.

I'm based in Madrid, where I explore different creative disciplines, from editorial design and branding to photography and moving images.

My approach is driven by curiosity, experimentation and a fascination with the relationship between images, ideas and emotions.

I believe that good design goes beyond aesthetics. It's about communicating something meaningful and finding beauty in the details.
